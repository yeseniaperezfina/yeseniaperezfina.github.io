The Echo
Jar

A place for the questions that stay with me.

Much of my professional work asks me to make an idea useful, legible, or actionable. The Echo Jar is a different room. Here I can follow a thought before I know what it is for.

Science is part of how I see the world, but these pieces are not science explainers. They are conversations with wonder, memory, uncertainty, attention, and the ordinary things that become strange when we look long enough.

Why it belongs here

Attention is part of my work, even when the work has no deliverable.

The Echo Jar is not a case study and it does not need to become one. It is another record of how I think: through association, image, science, memory, and the questions that refuse to behave like bullet points.
