The Echo Jar

A place for the questions that stay with me.

Much of my work involves helping people find a way into an idea. Here, I give myself room to wander through one. The Echo Jar is my personal writing on Substack: a space for following a thought, noticing what it touches, and not always knowing where it will land.

Science is part of how I see the world, but these pieces are not explanations of it. They are a more personal conversation with wonder, uncertainty, and the ordinary things that ask us to look again.
