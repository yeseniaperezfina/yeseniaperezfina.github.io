What I keep returning to

Access is only the beginning. The question is whether people can move.

I care about the space between being admitted into a system and being able to make meaningful progress through it. That means looking closely at pathways, recognition, support, language, belonging, and the rules that quietly determine who has to work hardest to move forward.

I also care about institutional capacity. Good practice does not scale because someone writes a better slogan. It scales when people have usable tools, clear decisions, trusted relationships, appropriate resources, and evidence strong enough to improve the next round.
