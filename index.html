Hello. I’m glad you’re here.

I’m Yesenia Pérez.

I build learning experiences, partnerships, and systems that help people find their place inside complex ideas.

My work has taken me through museums, NASA missions, national educator networks, public gardens, and higher education. The settings change. The questions stay surprisingly consistent: Who gets invited in? What helps people participate with confidence? What must an institution build around a good idea for it to truly hold?

What I keep returning to

People can feel when a system was designed with them in mind.

I care about the moment when something difficult becomes legible without becoming small. That might mean helping an educator translate new astronomy for a local audience, designing a clearer route through an interactive product, or building the support around a program so that people can use it without having to reverse-engineer the institution first.

I am most at home in work that asks for both imagination and infrastructure. The visible experience matters. So do the relationships, budgets, decisions, feedback loops, and acts of maintenance that make the experience possible.

Webb digital interpretation

Finding a way through an image

For the first Webb STORIMap, I helped the team think beyond features and toward the audience’s actual journey: what they would notice, where they might get lost, and what the experience should help them understand.
