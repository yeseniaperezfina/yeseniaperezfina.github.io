How I work · The connective tissue

I design for the whole program, not only the curriculum.

A learning experience succeeds when its people, policies, resources, evidence, and routes to participation are strong enough to carry the academic promise.

  1. 01

    See the system

    Map the learners, staff, incentives, constraints, risks, and decision pathways around the work.

  2. 02

    Design for delivery

    Translate academic vision into outcomes, roles, tools, calendars, and standards people can execute.

  3. 03

    Build capacity

    Treat educators and partners as knowledgeable co-designers, then give them the structure and support to lead.

  4. 04

    Use evidence to decide

    Let data and reflection shape what is strengthened, scaled, redesigned, or brought to a thoughtful close.
