Program strategy · Field building · Public impact

I’m Yesenia Pérez.

I’m an educator and program leader working to make science and learning more accessible, meaningful, and connected to people’s lives.

Across NASA missions, museums, and higher education, I bring people and ideas together—and build the programs, partnerships, and support that help them do good work. That means setting direction, stewarding resources, and learning alongside the communities the work is meant to serve.

What I keep returning to

People can feel when a system was designed with them in mind.

I care about the moment when something difficult becomes legible without becoming small. That might mean helping an educator translate new astronomy for a local audience, designing a clearer route through an interactive product, or building the support around a program so that people can use it without having to reverse-engineer the institution first.

I am most at home in work that asks for both imagination and infrastructure. The visible experience matters. So do the relationships, budgets, decisions, feedback loops, and acts of maintenance that make the experience possible.
