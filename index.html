Practice / 00

I design the conditions that help people and institutions move.

I’m Yesenia Pérez, an education strategist, program leader, researcher, and writer working across higher education, NASA science education, museums, and national partner networks.

I work on the space between an ambitious idea and an experience people can actually use. That means following learners, partners, decisions, resources, evidence, and the rules that determine whether progress continues or stops.

Practice / 01

The throughline is movement.

Not movement for its own sake. Movement with enough structure that people do not have to rely on heroics.

01

People

What does someone need to enter, understand, and keep going?

Audience strategy, belonging, interpretation, learning pathways, and usable choices.

02

Institutions

Which rules, decisions, and resources make a promise real?

Recognition, governance, program strategy, funding, implementation, and accountability.

03

Networks

How does knowledge travel without flattening local expertise?

Partner infrastructure, professional learning, field-building, facilitation, and trust.

04

Evidence

What happened, what changed, and what should the next version learn?

Evaluation, audience research, implementation learning, reflection, and iteration.

05

Possibility

What becomes possible when the system carries more of the load?

Durable pathways, confident hosts, clearer products, stronger partnerships, and wider participation.

Question / recurring

Access is only the beginning. The question is whether people can move.

Across transfer pathways, national science networks, digital interpretation, and community activation, I keep returning to the same design problem: where does momentum depend on an individual navigating around the system, and where can the system change instead?
