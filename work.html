Harvard Graduate School of Education · Completed 2026

When does learning actually count?

My graduate research examined apprenticeship-to-degree pathways, recognition, transfer, and the institutional rules that determine whether validated learning becomes usable credential progress. The work resulted in an Applied-Credit Reliability Diagnostic for locating where pathway promises become discretionary.
